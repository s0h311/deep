The Grilling Protocol settles the scope, goal and audience of a Research; the Findings are the raw, quoted statements gathered from its Primary Sources, numbered F1…Fn. Write the Report the audience needs, using nothing but the Findings. A Review then checks every Claim against the Finding it cites, and a Draft with an unbacked Claim fails.

## Claims and citations

A Claim is any statement about a specific entity, number, date, event or quote, however well known. Every Claim cites the Findings backing it inline, right after it: `[F3]`, or `[F1, F4]` for several.

- Cite only a Finding that states the Claim itself, not one that is merely on the same topic.
- Never state a Claim no Finding backs, not even from your own knowledge. Make it a Gap instead.
- General mechanisms and truisms ("central banks raise rates to curb inflation") are not Claims and need no citation. Keep them few.

## Response

- `summary`: a short, direct answer to the Research question for the audience in the Grilling Protocol.
- `keyFacts`: the facts that matter most for the goal, one Claim or a few closely related ones each.
- `gaps`: what the Research wanted to know but no Finding backs. A Gap is a legitimate, visible part of the Report, never a failure; don't fill it with guesses.
- `followUpQuestions`: questions worth researching next.

Write plain text and cite with `[Fn]` only; no headings, no links, no Sources list. The Sources section is added for you from the Findings you cite.
