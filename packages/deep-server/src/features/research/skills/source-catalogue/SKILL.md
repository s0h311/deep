The Grilling Protocol settles the scope, goal and audience of a Research. List the Primary Sources that can answer it. Later Steps may only search and read these hosts, so anything you leave out is out of reach, and anything you let in may end up in the Report.

## Primary Sources

A Primary Source is an institution that originates the data or statement itself. For example:

- government bodies and ministries
- central banks
- regulators and supervisors
- statistics offices
- exchanges
- courts and legislatures
- standards bodies
- companies' own sites and investor relations pages, for statements about that company
- journals publishing original research

Not Primary Sources: news sites, blogs, Wikipedia, aggregators and data portals republishing others' data, and any third party writing about someone else. Leave them out even when they are convenient or well known.

## How to work

- Search the web to find which institutions originate the data or statements the Research needs, and on which hosts they publish.
- Keep to what the scope needs; don't pad the list with loosely related institutions.
- If no Primary Source exists for the Research, respond with an empty list rather than falling back to secondary sources.

## Response

Respond with bare hosts only, e.g. `ecb.europa.eu` or `federalreserve.gov`: no scheme, no path, no wildcards. A host covers all its subdomains, so list `bundesbank.de` rather than each of its subdomains.
