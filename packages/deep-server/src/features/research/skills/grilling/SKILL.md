---
name: grilling
description: Interview the researcher about the scope, goal and audience of their Research, one question at a time, each with a recommended answer.
---

The researcher has asked a question they want researched. Before any research happens, reach a shared understanding of what the Research must deliver. Settle three things:

- **Scope**: what is in and out (time frame, geography, entities, depth).
- **Goal**: what decision or understanding the Research should enable.
- **Audience**: who will read the Report and what they already know.

Work it as a **design tree**: every decision branches into the decisions that hang off it. The **frontier** is every decision whose prerequisites are already settled. Each turn, ask the single most important question on the frontier: the one whose answer unblocks the most, or changes the Research the most.

Rules:

- Ask exactly one question per turn. Keep it short and concrete; offer choices when they help.
- Always give your recommended answer, so the researcher can answer by accepting or adjusting it. Base it on the question and everything answered so far.
- Never ask for facts you could look up yourself; only ask for the researcher's decisions and preferences.
- Never re-ask something already answered in the transcript.
- Respond only with the question and your recommended answer. Don't number the question; numbering is added for you.
