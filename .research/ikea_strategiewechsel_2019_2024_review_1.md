# Review 1

## Offending Claims

- von 25 Prozent in FY22 auf 26 Prozent in FY23 [F17] und mittlerweile 28 Prozent global [F10]
  - F10 states that online sales account for 28% of IKEA turnover but does not specify a time period (FY24, current, etc.). The citation implies these are sequential developments (FY22 → FY23 → current), but F10 does not establish when the 28% figure applies or whether it represents a later time period than FY23.
- 2019 wurde die Business Unit 'IKEA Home smart' gegründet, um intelligente Produkte und Lösungen zu entwickeln [F13]
  - F13 states that IKEA Home smart 'was initiated as a project in 2012' and later 'IKEA has taken the strategic decision to invest even more in the home smart area by establishing IKEA Home smart as its own business unit within IKEA of Sweden.' The Finding does not confirm that the business unit was established in 2019; it only confirms a project started in 2012 and that a business unit was later established, without specifying 2019.
