# Review 2

## Offending Claims

- IKEA hat sich vom 70 Jahre lang erfolgreichen gedruckten Katalog verabschiedet [F1, F8]
  - F1 and F8 both confirm IKEA ended the printed catalog, but neither specifies it was 70 years old in their statements. F8 states 'the successful career of the IKEA Catalog' ended, but doesn't explicitly mention '70 years'. This claim conflates information from the source title/context with what is actually stated in the findings.
- Der Online-Anteil am Geschäft ist deutlich gestiegen – von 25 Prozent in FY22 auf 26 Prozent in FY23 [F17]
  - F17 only mentions the FY22 to FY23 change (25% in FY22 to 26% in FY23). However, the claim uses the phrase 'deutlich gestiegen' (significantly increased), which is not supported by the data showing only a 1 percentage point increase.
