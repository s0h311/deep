# Review 1

## Offending Claims

- Der DAX begann das Jahr 2026 auf Rekordhöhen über 25.000 Punkte mit einem starken Vorjahresanstieg von 23 Prozent [F7]
  - F7 says the DAX 'notierte erstmals über der Marke von 25.000 Punkten' (traded for the first time above 25,000 points), but does not provide the previous year's growth rate of 23 percent. The claim conflates two separate pieces of information.
- welche im April 2026 nach gescheiterten Friedensverhandlungen über 102 USD pro Barrel Brent erreichten [F6]
  - F6 states oil prices 'is again significantly increased, the barrel Brent costs on Monday morning 102 US dollars' but does not explicitly state this occurred 'after failed peace negotiations' or in April 2026 as a result. The quote mentions failed negotiations but does not support that these prices were reached 'after' failed negotiations or that April 2026 is when they were at this level.
- Sie hielt die Leitzinsen am 19. März 2026 unverändert [F5]
  - F5 does not contain any information about holding interest rates unchanged. It only discusses the consequences of the Middle East war with upside risks to inflation and downside risks to growth.
- erhöhte sie aber später um 25 Basispunkte am 11. Juni 2026 aufgrund des Inflationsdrucks [F4]
  - F4 states the rate increase was due to 'the Middle East War' and resulting inflation pressure ('Krieg im Nahen Osten' und 'Druck auf die Inflation'), not just 'Inflationsdrucks'. The claim partially omits the war connection mentioned in F4.
- Der schwache US-Arbeitsmarktbericht für Juli 2026 senkte die Zinserwartungen an die Fed deutlich und trieb den DAX auf Rekordhöhen [F9]
  - F9 mentions the weak US labor market report and reduced interest rate expectations, but does not explicitly state it 'lowered Fed interest rate expectations' with that specific attribution or that it 'drove the DAX to record highs'. The quote shows correlation, not the causal relationship claimed.
